## About me

I lead development and write code myself: I take products from an idea to a working MVP and production, and grow
together with the team. I have **4+ years of development experience**, and before that I spent **11 years running my own
business**, so for every task I ask not only "how to build it" but also "why do people and the business need it".

- **Built a B2B freight platform from scratch in 3 months** with a team of 7: a marketplace where customers find
  carriers, and companies work safely with drivers, vehicles and documents. Designed the microservice architecture and
  wrote the key services: company profiles, back office, single sign-on, role-based access, notifications (push and
  email), reviews and ratings, documents, and an integration with the Saby (SBIS) electronic document API.
- **Production-ready delivery:** infrastructure on four servers compliant with the Russian data-protection law
  (152-FZ), a five-stage CI/CD pipeline with security scanning, monitoring, and **80%+ test coverage**.
- **AI automation:** a real-time voice AI avatar with lip sync that takes a person from the first look at a product
  to registration in 2-3 minutes; a status-management assistant that cut project managers' routine work by **50%**;
  an agent factory and RAG tooling on LangChain and LangGraph.
- **Search at scale:** a parser and search over **10M+ documents** on Elasticsearch and MongoDB with configurable
  notifications.
- **Team first:** code reviews, mentoring, sprint planning and architecture discussions.
